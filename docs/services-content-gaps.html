Implementation & Asset Ingestion Standards

1. Metric Verification Rule

Never publish invented statistics (e.g. "98.4% success rate"). All performance claims must be backed by documented CRM outreach logs and signed event confirmations.

  • Retain copies of organizer booking confirmations.
  • Maintain client permission on all published fee brackets.

2. Speaker Roster Ingestion

When onboarding new featured speakers to `/services/` and `/pricing/`, collect standard media kit assets:

  • High-resolution stage photography (min 2400x1600px).
  • Signed testimonial release or verified LinkedIn quote.
  • Link to live personal brand site or LinkedIn profile.

3. Web Design Delivery SLA

To sustain the $1,100 fixed price without scope creep, provide clients with the standardized Brux Web Intake Form before coding begins:

  • Keynote topic titles, 3 learning takeaways per topic.
  • Direct embedding link for video reel (YouTube/Vimeo/MP4).
  • Google Calendar or Calendly booking appointment link.
